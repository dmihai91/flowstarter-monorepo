I BUILT MY PRACTICE ON ONE CONVICTION: STRATEGY MUST EXECUTE

About Me

I work with leaders who are serious about the hard part — not just designing strategy, but building the conditions in which it can actually be executed. That means getting into the organisation, understanding what's really going on, and telling you what I see.

I came up through McKinsey, where I spent seven years working on strategy and transformation engagements across Europe, North America, and Southeast Asia. I left in 2016 to build my own practice — smaller, more focused, more direct.

My clients are usually CEOs, founders, and leadership teams at an inflection point: a major acquisition, a growth plateau, a transition from founder-led to professionally managed, a market that's changed faster than the business. The work is different every time. The discipline is the same.

OUTSIDE THE BOARDROOM

I live in London with my partner and our two enormous dogs. I run — badly but consistently — and read obsessively across history, economics, and biography. I am constitutionally unable to read only one book at a time.

I also mentor early-career consultants and write occasionally about strategy and leadership on Substack. The writing keeps me honest about what I actually think.

EXPERIENCE & APPROACH

My McKinsey years gave me exposure to an enormous range of strategic situations — across sectors, geographies, and scales. What I took from that period isn't a methodology. It's a habit of diagnosis. Of questioning the framing before engaging with the content.

Since going independent, I've worked with organisations where the stakes are real and the time for theatre has passed. The work is harder and more honest. I prefer it.

MY CAREER IN BRIEF

The engagements and turning points that shaped how I think about strategy and execution.

Read Economics at Oxford. Joined McKinsey after graduating — initially as an analyst in the London office, covering financial services and retail.

Seconded to McKinsey's Southeast Asia practice for 18 months. Worked on market-entry and growth strategy engagements in Singapore, Indonesia, and Vietnam.

Returned to LBS for an MBA — a deliberate pause to think about what I actually wanted to build. Graduated with Distinction. Met the people I still think of as my best thinking partners.

Left McKinsey to start my own practice. The first year was uncomfortable and clarifying. I worked with three clients, all referrals, all repeat engagements within 12 months.

The pandemic forced — and then validated — fully remote delivery on engagements across 4 continents. Adapted the practice accordingly. Some things are better remote. The diagnostics aren't.

A decade in. The work is more selective, more focused, and more satisfying than I expected. I turn down more than I take on — which makes what I do take on worth doing properly.
