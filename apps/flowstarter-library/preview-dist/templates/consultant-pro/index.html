How I Work

MY CONSULTING APPROACH

01

Diagnostic Clarity First

Most engagements start in the wrong place because the problem statement is wrong. I invest heavily upfront in understanding what's actually going on — not just what's been said about it.

02

Stakeholder Alignment as Strategy

A perfect strategy that the leadership team doesn't believe in is worthless. I build alignment into the process — not as an afterthought, but as a core deliverable.

03

Design for Execution

Strategy documents are not strategies. I design with the people who will implement, building in the feedback loops and decision rights that let things actually move.

04

Honest Counsel, Not Just Validation

I'll tell you what I see — including the things the organisation doesn't want to hear. That's not comfortable. It's what you're paying for.

05

Transfer, Don't Create Dependency

I build capability in the teams I work with. The measure of a good engagement is whether the organisation can sustain the work after I leave.

Philosophy

WHERE CLARITY MEETS COMMITTED ACTION

Strategy without execution is theatre. Execution without strategy is exhausting. The work is finding the intersection — and making it real enough that people can actually walk into it.

Strategy Sprint

Intensive 2–4 week engagement. Diagnose the core problem, define the strategic options, build the 90-day action plan. For when you need clarity fast.

Fractional CSO

Strategic leadership without the full-time hire. I embed in your leadership team for 1–2 days per week — present for the decisions that matter.

Post-Merger Integration

Integration design and programme management for complex M&A situations. Culture, operations, leadership — the parts that determine whether it works.

Board & Investor Advisory

Strategic counsel at board level. Preparing for fundraising, navigating investor relationships, and ensuring the board has the information it needs to be useful.

Leadership Team Workshops

Facilitated strategy sessions and alignment workshops for leadership teams. From annual planning to conflict resolution to decision-making frameworks.

Interim Strategic Leadership

For organisations in transition that need someone to hold the strategic function while they find or develop permanent leadership.
