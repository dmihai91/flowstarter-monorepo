YOU'VE GOT QUESTIONS I'VE GOT THE ANSWERS

What kinds of projects do you usually help with?

I usually step in when a product needs clearer UX, a stronger interface system, or a more structured path through complex features, dashboards, websites, and digital products.

When should a team bring you in?

The earlier, the better. But I also help when teams are already stuck, launches are drifting, or the product feels harder to use than it should.

Do you work on both UX and visual design?

Yes. I move from product thinking and flow definition into interface design, systems, and the detail work that makes a product feel coherent and ready to ship.

Can you help with design systems and handoff?

Yes. I build structured UI systems, reusable patterns, and handoff-ready files that help design and development stay aligned instead of reinventing the same pieces repeatedly.

How do you usually collaborate with teams?

I work closely with founders, product managers, and developers. The goal is to keep decisions clear, feedback fast, and the design process useful instead of heavy.

What do you need from us to get started?

A short view of the product, the current blockers, and what success should look like is usually enough to begin. From there, I can help shape the right next steps.
