Process

MY EXPERTISE & PHOTOGRAPHIC APPROACH

01

Natural Light Mastery

Understanding how light behaves — golden hour, window light, overcast diffusion — and using it to sculpt images that feel alive rather than lit.

02

Candid Storytelling

The best images are rarely posed. I create environments where authentic moments emerge, then I'm ready when they do.

03

Technical Precision

Exposure, focus, composition, and timing — the technical fundamentals that separate a good frame from a great one.

04

Post-Production

Retouching and colour grading that enhances the image without overworking it — keeping the photograph honest to the moment.

05

Client Direction

Helping people feel comfortable in front of the camera, translating briefs into visual language, and keeping shoots on time without losing spontaneity.

About Me

For the past 8 years I've photographed brands, weddings, people, and places — always with the same belief that every frame matters.

I'm based in Berlin but my work has taken me across Europe, into editorial studios, and into the lives of people generous enough to trust me with their stories.

Photography taught me to slow down, pay attention, and find the truth hiding in plain sight. That's a lesson I bring to every shoot.
