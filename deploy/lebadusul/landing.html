About this workspace

Lebădușul is a Romanian fishing-tackle storefront on Shopify. This Flowstarter workspace is the operator surface for the team — visit the live store, open the AI-paired editor on the draft theme, or preview pending changes before publishing.

Changes made in the editor never auto-publish: they sit on the draft theme until Publish is explicitly clicked, then go live via shopify theme push. The live store stays exactly as it was until then.
