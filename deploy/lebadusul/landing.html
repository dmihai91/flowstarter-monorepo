About this workspace

Lebădușul is a Romanian fishing-tackle storefront on Shopify. This page is the operator surface for the workspace, one place to visit, edit with the AI pair-editor, or preview pending changes.

Changes made through the editor never auto-publish. They sit on the draft theme until you click publish, then ship via shopify theme push. Until that moment, the live store stays exactly as it is.
