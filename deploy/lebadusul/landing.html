On this workspace

Lebădușul — the little swan — is a Romanian fishing-tackle storefront on Shopify. This page is the Flowstarter operator surface for the workspace: a single leaf you open to visit, edit, or preview, with the AI pair-editor sat one click away on the draft theme.

Changes made through the editor are never auto-published; they sit on the draft theme until publish is clicked, then ship via shopify theme push. The live store stays exactly as it was until that moment.
